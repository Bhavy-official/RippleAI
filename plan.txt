```text
       ↓
    Recover
       │
       ↓
    Replay
```

**If time starts running out, never cut the middle of this pipeline.** Cut optional UI polish, AI explanation, AWS extras, or future-facing functionality first.

And the most important architectural decision in the prompt is this:

> **Statistics/rules detect the anomaly → correlation creates the incident → AI explains the evidence.**

That keeps Ripple Ai technically credible instead of becoming another “send logs to an LLM” project.
